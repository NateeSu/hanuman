# Art Direction

“Cinematic Thai mural fantasy”: Thai khon and mural motifs, painterly cinematic light, crisp silhouettes, readable mobile-scale detail, no pixel art, chibi, anime-school styling, Western superhero suits or photoreal cosplay.

Hero colors: white, warm gold, silver and wind cyan.  
Maiyarap: deep violet, black, copper gold and poisonous green.  
Level 1: moon blue, smoky purple and torch gold.  
Level 2: teal water, emerald, lotus pink and cave indigo.  
Level 3: black-violet, storm gold, crimson warning and green heart magic.

All images: no text, no logo, no watermark, no duplicated limbs, clear gameplay silhouettes.
