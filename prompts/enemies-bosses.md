# Enemies and Bosses

Bosses use the shared roster prompt and Thai mural fantasy bible:

- Night Gatekeeper: heavy indigo-black yaksha, copper-gold armor, spear and shield.
- Matchanu: aqua-green monkey/fish guardian, gold ornaments and trident; strong rather than villainous.
- Maiyarap: black-violet sorcerer king, tall crown, staff and green magic.

Enemy silhouettes must remain distinct: compact melee guard, lean archer, winged spirit, robed mage.
