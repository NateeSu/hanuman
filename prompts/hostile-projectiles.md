# Hostile Projectiles

All assets use `stylized-concept` and the corresponding enemy sprite as a style/palette reference. Characters are excluded from the output. Each projectile is isolated on a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key background with crisp separated edges, generous padding, no text, no watermark, and no cast/contact shadow.

## Yak arrow

A single long enchanted Thai demon arrow flying horizontally right: black-metal shaft, ornate red-and-gold Ramakien arrowhead, dark fletching, and a restrained amber speed glint. It must remain readable at 90 pixels wide.

## Shadow Mage orb

A compact cursed fireball flying right: bright acid-green core wrapped in dark smoky black-green flame curls, with a short tapering tail. The solid core and silhouette must remain readable at 64 pixels.

## Bat Spirit bolt

A compact crescent-shaped spirit scream flying right: blue-violet energy, cyan-white leading edge, and dark indigo inner wisps resembling supernatural bat sonar. It must remain readable at 70 pixels.

## Boss ground wave

A low, wide magical shockwave traveling right along the ground: antique gold and poisonous emerald energy, a narrow ground contact point, ornate Thai flame motifs, and a short smoky tail. It must remain readable at 120 pixels.
