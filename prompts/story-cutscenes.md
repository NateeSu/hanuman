# Story cutscene image prompts

All seven story illustrations were generated with the built-in Image Gen tool as
new, project-bound artwork. They share this production direction:

- `illustration-story`, full-screen 16:9 landscape
- premium painterly Thai Ramakien fantasy key art
- refined Thai mural and khon design language with cinematic realism
- darker, quieter left 42% reserved for native UI story text
- narrative action in the right 58%
- no reused game assets, collage, split panels, UI, text, logo, or watermark

## Scene briefs

1. **Rama's camp:** Maiyarap carries the sleeping Rama through the underworld
   gate while Hanuman awakens among the enchanted vanara army.
2. **Elephant gorge:** Hanuman leaps across a subterranean canyon toward a
   formation of enchanted war elephants and their colossal guardian.
3. **Clashing mountains:** Hanuman races through a split-second opening between
   black volcanic cliffs while the fire-stone sentinel guards the molten core.
4. **Demon mosquito wilds:** Hanuman's cyan wind parts the mosquito queen's
   swarm and reveals the luminous lotus path. No Rama or additional hero.
5. **Lotus pool:** Hanuman and the fish-tailed Matchanu meet in a balanced duel
   whose mirrored poses hint at their unknown father-son bond.
6. **Iron prison:** Hanuman reaches Rama's cage in the moonlit toddy grove,
   where three glowing seals and enchanted chains block the rescue.
7. **Hidden heart:** Hanuman's wind trishula strikes at Maiyarap's floating
   heart reliquary during their final duel. No Rama or additional hero.
