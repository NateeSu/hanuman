# Trishula Ultimate

Use case: stylized-concept  
Asset type: production 2D game weapon sprite for the special ultimate attack in an existing Thai fantasy side-scrolling action game  
Primary request: create one ornate divine trident (ตรีศูล) designed to be thrown like a magical returning weapon by Hanuman  
Subject: a single symmetrical three-pronged Thai mythological trident, long elegant gold-and-bronze shaft, sharp ivory-gold blades, restrained cyan-white wind energy inlays, small red enamel details that match Hanuman's red-and-gold costume  
Style/medium: polished high-end 2D game illustration, painterly fantasy rendering, crisp silhouette, strong readable shape at small scale, matching ornate Thai Ramakien character art  
Composition/framing: weapon perfectly horizontal pointing to the right, centered, isolated, full weapon visible with generous padding, no perspective distortion, no motion blur and no circular trail baked into the sprite  
Lighting/mood: luminous divine rim light, heroic and powerful  
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background for background removal; the background must be one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation  
Constraints: only one weapon; subject fully separated from background with crisp edges; do not use `#ff00ff` anywhere in the weapon; no cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection, no text, no watermark, no border, no hands, no character, no extra objects  
Avoid: photorealism, modern sci-fi, chunky proportions, cropped tips, soft fuzzy edges, purple or magenta glow
